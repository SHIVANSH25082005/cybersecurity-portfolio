4 [Normal]: 1. Introduction
5 [Normal]: Cybercrime investigations often involve large volumes of fragmented information including complaints, phone numbers, UPI IDs, email addresses, websites, IP addresses, and other digital indicators. Investigators must manually analyze these datasets to identify hidden relationships, shared infrastructure, and links between seemingly unrelated cases.
6 [Normal]: The Integrated Cybercrime Intelligence & Investigation Platform was developed as an investigation-support system to centralize cybercrime intelligence, manage case information, analyze IPDR records, discover hidden relationships, and correlate investigations through shared indicators. The platform combines intelligence management, analytical tools, relationship discovery, and graph-based visualization within a unified environment, enabling investigators to transform isolated records into actionable intelligence.
7 [Normal]: Objectives
8 [Normal]: Centralize cybercrime intelligence records and investigation data.
9 [Normal]: Support structured case-based investigation workflows.
10 [Normal]: Discover relationships through shared indicators and infrastructure.
11 [Normal]: Correlate investigations using common entities and IP addresses.
12 [Normal]: Visualize investigative networks through graph analysis.
13 [Normal (Web)]: Reduce false-positive correlations through confidence-based IP relationship analysis.
14 [Normal (Web)]: Explain investigative relationships through evidence-based correlation reasoning.

28 [Normal]: Investigative Recommendations
29 [Normal]: The Correlation Engine serves as a core analytical component of the platform. It compares information across complaints, intelligence records, IPDR datasets, and cases to identify common indicators and hidden relationships.
30 [Normal]: Relationship Explanation Engine
31 [Normal]: To improve investigative transparency, the platform includes a Relationship Explanation Engine that automatically explains why two cases are correlated. For each discovered relationship, investigators are investigators are provided with the underlying evidence including shared indicators such as phone numbers, UPI IDs, email addresses, websites, shared IP infrastructure, fan-out analysis results, time proximity findings, and confidence assessments. Rather than presenting correlations as simple links, the platform provides investigative reasoning and supporting evidence for every identified relationship. This enables investigators to understand not only that a relationship exists, but also the reasoning behind it..

93 [Normal]: 4. Graph Analysis & Visualization
94 [Normal]: Graph-based visualization simplifies complex investigative relationships and enables investigators to understand large datasets more effectively.
95 [Normal]: Interactive Visualization Enhancements
96 [Normal]: All analytical visualization layers across the platform—including the Intelligence Fusion Graph, the Link Analysis Graph, and the Case Correlation Graph—have been modernized to support native interactivity. Investigators can zoom, pan, toggle fullscreen modes, drag nodes, and export graphs directly to PNG or SVG formats. To maintain absolute visual fidelity and pixel-perfect layouts matching NetworkX calculations, the system overlays high-performance interactive controls over static Matplotlib renders, incorporating click coordinate logging.

113 [Normal]: Geo-spatial Intelligence Mapping
114 [Normal]: The Geo-spatial Intelligence module integrates interactive spatial telemetry mapping within the platform. Built using Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap, it maps location telemetry from complaints, intelligence profiles, and cell tower telemetry logs. Locations are plotted dynamically on a dark-themed interactive map with support for marker clustering and heatmap layers to identify density hotspots.
115 [Normal]: If coordinate fields are missing during record entry, the platform executes a single geocoding call via OpenStreetMap Nominatim on form submission and persists the coordinates. While rendering the map, it retrieves coordinates directly from the database without invoking external APIs, optimizing load performance. The page includes a statistics summary panel tracking total mapped and missing locations, country/state/city coverage, and multi-parameter filters.

Confidence scores are derived using a combination of shared infrastructure analysis, fan-out behavior, and temporal proximity between connected entities.
